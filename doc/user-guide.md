## 3. Section-by-Section Interactive Guide

### 01 • Minutes of Previous Meeting
- Scroll through 6 action items from the previous meeting.
- Each card shows the commitment, current status (`Closed`, `Ongoing`, `Open`), and next steps.
- Click any action card to see its full story detail overlay.

### 02 • IT Operations

**Left panel — KPI Metric Cards:**
- Click any of the 5 KPI cards (`Availability`, `CSAT`, `Change`, `Major Incidents`, `KPI Health`) to highlight and filter the trend chart to that metric.
- The selected month's data populates the hero number.

**Trend Chart (right panel):**
- Line chart shows Apr–Jul actuals + projected Aug–Sep.
- Hover over any data point to see the exact value.

**Month Selector:**
- Use the month tab row (`Apr`, `May`, `Jun`, `Jul`, `Aug`, `Sep`) to view a specific month's KPI summary.

**Executive Notes:**
- Click the ✏️ edit icon to type custom executive commentary directly onto the slide.
- Notes are saved locally and persist across sessions.

**Ticket Tab:**
- Switch to the Ticket sub-view to see the full 4-month ticket closure trend, team volumetrics (Service Desk / Infra / EUS / Security), and top incident and SR categories.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I present this offline without internet access?**  
**A:** Yes. The platform contains complete offline datasets. All visuals render without SharePoint or internet connectivity.

**Q: How is the CSAT score shown?**  
**A:** As a raw score out of 5.00 (e.g., `4.54 / 5.00`) matching the IT Operations dashboard display. The target is `4.50`.

**Q: Which quarter is currently active in the AIOps Roadmap?**  
**A:** **Q4 2026** — highlighted in red/amber with a live beacon indicator and a centered vertical milestone line.

**Q: What does "Exclusions Approved" mean in Vulnerability Management?**  
**A:** Vulnerabilities that have been reviewed, assessed as acceptable risk or remediated by policy, and formally approved for exclusion from the open count.

**Q: How do I close drilldown modals?**  
**A:** Click the **"Close"** button at the bottom or top-right of any modal.

**Q: How do I switch between dark and light mode?**  
**A:** Use the theme toggle icon in the navigation header bar.
